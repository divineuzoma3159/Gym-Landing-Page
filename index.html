Advisements

Beginner-friendly guidance

When working out make sure you have a good form because clear progress comes when have a good form, make sure you're in a calorie-deficit and take rests in between sets.

Structured work-out plan

• Monday - Back + Lats + Arms

• Tuesday - Shoulders + Chest

• Wednesday - Legs + Abs

• Friday - Back and Lats + Arms

• Saturday - Shoulders + Chest

Supportive Environment

Most people do not like going to the gym because of thier anxieties that people will judge them but it's the opposite instead they help and encourage you, so there's no need for hesitation.

Track your progress

When working out it is advisable to track your workout and your meals in order to be aware of how many calories you're taking if you're in a calorie deficit.
